- Each server has 9 base plots. On the plot-select screen, the < and > buttons cycle through them, and WASD does nothing there.
- Loading a save is limited to once every 60 seconds. If you load sooner, a popup shows instead.
- If another player loads onto the plot you picked at the same moment, you can spawn at Wood R Us instead of your base.
- Saving into an occupied slot shows a "Delete all data on this slot and replace with current progress?" confirmation first.
- Spook trees (the October event tree) have thick, smooth, curved, near-black limbs, like bent pipes or tentacles, not thin twigs. In screenshots their darkest pixels sit at luma 14–39 with almost no colour.
- Not verified, so don't state it as fact: the day/night cycle length (community figures say about 8 minutes of night and 16 minutes of day), and the rule that spook trees need a server several hours old.
